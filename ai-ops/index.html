How it works

  1. The audit 2 weeks · fixed price

    I read your data across every location and sort the problems into a few buckets. Each owner or GM gets the 3 things to do next, not a dashboard. It's yours whether or not we keep going, and the fee is credited to the first month if we do.

  2. Pick the number week 3

    Together we choose the one number to move: labor as a share of revenue, days to get paid, cost per hire, close rate, or hours of manual re-keying between systems. Then I write down how your best operator makes the calls behind it. That judgment becomes the rules.

  3. Build it and run it 90 days

    The smallest system that moves the number, live in your locations, not in a demo. It checks itself every night against your source systems. People keep the calls that need judgment. You get one written update a week, with the number on top.

  4. Keep going or hand it over after day 90

    Month to month on 30 days' notice. Everything is documented as it's built, so you can stop, move to the next number, or have me help you hire the person who runs it in-house.

Questions people ask

What if you're unavailable?

Everything is documented as it's built and the system checks itself overnight, so it doesn't depend on me being in the room. If you'd rather own it in-house, I'll help you hire the person who takes it over.

We run on different systems across locations. Does that rule us out?

No, that's often where the money is. The audit maps every location's back office, shows where the numbers disagree, and picks a number that can be moved without re-platforming first. Titan runs on Jobber today; for other systems I connect through their APIs, and the audit confirms what's reachable before you commit to more.

Where does our data go?

It stays in your systems. I work through access you grant and can revoke at any time, and nothing is copied out that the work doesn't need.

We already bought AI tools, or have a firm helping us.

Fine. I own the number, not the vendor list. I'll use what you've already paid for where it moves the number, and say plainly where it doesn't.
