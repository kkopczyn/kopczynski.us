Concept preview Chiefhouse

Run your day from one place.

Chiefhouse is a small team of AI agents that works as your chief of staff. Oli, the chief of staff, keeps the team on track. They do the legwork. You make the calls.

Your day

A rhythm you can count on

  • Morning briefing

    What's on today, what's waiting on you, and what slipped.

  • Evening wrap

    What got done, what's still open, and what moves to tomorrow.

  • Week ahead

    The week on one page, with clashes flagged before they happen.

In between, the team does the work.

  • Drafts. Replies, notes and documents, ready for your yes.
  • Tracks promises. Anything you or an agent said would happen, and when. Late ones show up in your briefing.
  • Sorts the calendar. Spots conflicts and proposes the fix.
  • Handles the inbox and follow-ups. Nothing quietly falls through.

How it stays safe

Nothing goes out without you

  • You approve every outward action, with a passkey on your phone.
  • Routine items clear in one tap. Your attention goes where it matters.
  • Agents can't approve themselves. A separate checker agent reviews the work before it reaches you.
  • Private data stays private. Your information is yours, kept apart from everyone else's.
  • Everything is logged. Most changes can be undone.

How you reach it
