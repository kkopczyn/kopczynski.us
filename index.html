What runs every day

I built Titan, an AI operating system for home services companies, and ran it across 120+ locations in two franchise systems. In my own two-location company, an AI operations agent now works next to the GMs on top of it, reading our field-service software, phone lines, shared inbox and crew WhatsApp groups. A day looks like this:

  • 3am, data check. Field-service data is reconciled overnight, so the morning numbers are right.
  • 5am, the numbers. Every manager and salesperson gets yesterday's results against target. Field labor went from 50% to 30% of revenue once managers saw dollars per van every morning.
  • 8:15, closeout. Were yesterday's jobs closed out and invoiced? The agent reads job notes and messages first, so it only nudges when something is actually missing.
  • 8:30, unpaid invoices. Follow-up texts go out, except to anyone with a complaint, a dispute, a cash payment or a recent text. Nearly 100% of invoices get collected, and about $20K of bad debt was recovered.
  • 9am and 4pm, GM follow-through. Before it flags a slow reply, the agent checks whether the work already got done.
  • During the day, dispatch. The GM books new jobs next to existing work and pulls future jobs into slots that open up.
  • 6pm, what's left. Every visit from today that still needs invoicing or payment.
  • Any time someone applies. They get a text within minutes, a short screening conversation and an interview booked with the GM as soon as possible. Cost per hire fell from about $1,000 to $100 to $150.
  • Mondays, the week ahead. The weekly report, plus appointment confirmations. One Monday the agent found 95 visits and sent 27 confirmations after a single approval.

Messages to customers go out after a person approves them, on the same software, phone lines and group chats the team already uses. See a full day in the case study or watch the 6-minute walkthrough.
