About Kingston Crew

A no-ads, live guide to the City of Kingston, NY: a map you can browse, a number you can text, and a Thursday email with the five best things to do this weekend.

The manifesto

  1. No ads. Ever.

    Nobody pays to be on this map, and nobody pays to be in the email. Places are here because they're worth the trip, not because they paid. We pay for the whole thing by making things worth buying.

  2. No politics. No crime.

    Other people cover the news, and some of them do it well. We cover where to eat on Friday and which room is loud on Saturday.

  3. Only the best of Kingston.

    This isn't a directory. We would rather list 80 places we'd stand behind than 800 we wouldn't. If something slips, it comes off the map.

  4. Software does the plumbing. People write the words.

    Software does the plumbing: calendars, the map, the text bot. Every word a subscriber reads will be written or rewritten by a person before launch. This preview is a first cut. When you text us, a bot answers. It's labeled as a bot, it only recommends what's on the map or in a credited listing, and it'll tell you when it doesn't know.

  5. We credit our sources.

    Most of what's on in Kingston, someone else listed first: venues, Kingston Happenings, Chronogram, Hudson Valley One, Catskill Crew. Every listing says where it came from, and we link back.

  6. We're neighbors first.

    We buy local, we print local where we can, and we show up. We'd like to be a chapter of Catskill Crew, never a rival to it.

FAQ

Who runs this?

Konrad, a Kingston resident, edits it, as a proposed chapter of Catskill Crew. Independent. Not affiliated with the City of Kingston. We're not the city, its tourism office, or any business on the map.

What is Kingston Crew?

It's a no-ads, live guide to the City of Kingston, NY. There's a map of what's good right now, a Thursday email with the five best things to do that weekend, and a number you can text with questions. We've proposed it as a chapter of Catskill Crew, which covers the whole Catskills and Hudson Valley region on Tuesdays.

Who picks what's on the map?

Right now, mostly software, and we'd rather say so. Places were researched and checked for Kingston; events are pulled live from local calendars like Kingston Happenings and the venues' own, and credited to each source. Software does the plumbing: calendars, the map, the text bot. Every word a subscriber reads will be written or rewritten by a person before launch. This preview is a first cut.

Is the text line a real person?

No. It's Wick, a bot, and it says so. Wick only recommends places on our map and events from credited listings, and it'll say "I don't know" before it guesses. Text HUMAN and a real person (Konrad) will get back to you, usually within a day.

How do you make money without ads?

Merch: limited drops like the Contour series, brick-stamp coasters and First Capital crewnecks, which we plan to print locally. Nobody pays to be listed, and nobody pays to be in the email. That's the whole point.

How often will you text or email me?

Email comes once a week, on Thursday at 4pm. Texts are capped at 2 a week, plus replies when you ask Wick something and any "text me when it's on" alerts you set yourself. Reply STOP anytime to stop texts, or unsubscribe from email with one click.

What do you do with my number and email?

We use them to send you Kingston Crew, and that's all. We never sell or share them. With no advertisers, there's no one to sell them to anyway.

Why no politics or crime?

Plenty of good outlets cover the news, and we're not one of them. Kingston Crew is about where to go and what to do. Wick will point you to local news sources if you ask about something outside its lane.

My place or event should be on here. How do I get it on?

Tell us: text the number or email us (reply to any Weekend Five). We can't promise a listing, and you can't pay for a spot. If you want to sponsor something, we'll send you to Catskill Crew, who do partnerships properly.

The small print

Privacy

We use your email and number to send you Kingston Crew, and that’s all. We never sell or share them; with no advertisers, there’s no one to sell them to. We count visits and sign-ups with PostHog, which is also where launch-list sign-ups are stored for now. Unsubscribe in one click or one text, and ask us to delete your details any time.

SMS terms

Kingston Crew texts are recurring automated marketing messages: picks, and the “tonight” alerts you ask for. Up to 2 msgs/week, plus replies when you text Wick and one text per “text me when it’s on” alert. Consent isn’t a condition of any purchase. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. The text line launches after carrier registration; until then nothing is sent.

Terms

Listings are picks, not promises: check with the venue before you go. Event details come from credited sources and can change. Nobody pays to be listed, and we can’t sell you a spot. Merch drops go to subscribers first; there’s no checkout on this site yet.
