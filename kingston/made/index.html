Made in Kingston

The breweries, bakers, roasters, makers and artists working in the city, what they make, and where to buy it. Same rules as the map: humans pick, nobody pays to be here.

Preview data: the full maker list is still being compiled.

TEN TO START WITH

The Made in Kingston ten

  1. 01Keegan AlesKingston’s original brewery, and a Midtown anchor for two decades.
  2. 02Kingston Bread + BarIts baguettes sell out by Sunday noon.
  3. 03Bailey Pottery EquipmentPotters around the world throw on Kingston-made wheels. The showroom holds a 500-piece ceramics collection.
  4. 04Lite Brite Neon StudioA hand-bent neon collective on Downs St. Very photogenic.
  5. 05Rowan WoodworkHand-tool woodworking, and the “Kingston Kez” hand-planing nights.
  6. 06Monkey Joe Roasting Co.Roasting in Midtown since 2000, and the city’s favorite cup.
  7. 07O+ Festival / Art at 334Murals in exchange for health care. Art at 334 is open Thursday to Saturday, 12 to 6.
  8. 08Kingston Ceramics StudioA clay studio on the third floor of the Shirt Factory. Take a class, leave with a mug.
  9. 09La Pequeña TextilesHandwoven in Kingston by a Spanish-Dominican weaver. Small, beautiful, giftable.
  10. 10Kingston Standard BrewingA tiny brewery with beers built on Kingston history.
