    Get the Weekend Five

    Once it launches: every Thursday, the five best things to do in Kingston that weekend, plus one new opening and one bit of history. Two minutes to read, no ads.

    Email: once a week. Texts: up to 2 a week, only for picks and reminders you asked for. Merch drops go to subscribers first.

    WHAT THIS IS

    One map, one number to text, one email on Thursdays.

    Kingston Crew is a no-ads, live guide to the City of Kingston: a map you can browse, a number you can text, and a Thursday email with the five best things to do this weekend. It’s run by a neighbor, it’s proposed as a chapter of Catskill Crew, and it would be paid for by merch, not sponsors.
