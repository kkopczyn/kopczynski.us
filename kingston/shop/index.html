The Capital Drop · Burning of Kingston week · Oct 15–17

Burned in 1777.Still here.

On Oct 16, 1777, the British burned New York’s first capital. The stone walls stood, and Kingston moved back in. The October drop is for the town that didn’t leave: the First Capital Crewneck, the brick-stamp coasters and a run of Still Here tees.

The Contour Crew Car

Not for sale

A partial wrap (sides and hood) in satin graphite, with the Contour pattern running toward the front of the car. No slogans, no logo slab. The only text is a small line near the rear wheel: “Pattern generated from Kingston’s terrain and rivers. See how →”, with a QR code to the How it was made page.
