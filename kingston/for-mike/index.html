Proposal (first cut, for your feedback)

Mike, this is the rough deal I have in mind for a Kingston chapter of Catskill Crew. Nothing here is final, and any of it can change.

The idea

A no-ads guide to the City of Kingston: a map of what's on this week, a Thursday email (never on your Tuesday) and a number people can text for picks. Merch pays for it, not ads. It runs as "Kingston Crew, a Catskill Crew chapter", only if you approve.

Terms

  • Pilot: 90 days. After that we decide together whether it keeps going.
  • Merch: 15% of Kingston Crew merch goes to Catskill Crew. Co-branded drops split 50/50 after costs.
  • The name: you approve anything that uses "Crew".
  • Tech: no cost to you. I host the map, the event feed and the text bot, and I pay the SMS fees during the pilot. You're welcome to use it for Catskill Crew too.
  • The list: shared. If either of us walks, we email everyone once and subscribers choose which they want to keep getting. Nobody gets moved without being asked.
  • Walking away: either of us can, any time. If you do, I drop the name within 30 days.
  • Ads and sponsors: none on our side, so we never compete for yours.

How we'd know it's working

At day 90 the targets are 1,500 email subscribers and 400 SMS sign-ups. You'd get the numbers weekly.
